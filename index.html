EXAM REVISION

English revision practice

Use this short page to review key language before and after exam week. Read the reminders, complete the practice, then check what to revisit.

Quick review

Articles: a / the

Use a for one thing in general: a book. Use the for a specific thing: the book on the desk.

Present simple

Positive: I play; she plays. Negative: do not / don’t, does not / doesn’t. Questions: Do you…? / Does he…?

Contractions

I’m, you’re, he’s, we’re; don’t, doesn’t. In a negative present-simple sentence, use doesn’t + base verb.

Frequency & time

always, usually, often, sometimes, never. Say in the morning, at 7 o’clock, on Monday.

Remember: With he / she / it, add -s in a positive sentence: She reads. But after doesn’t or does, use the base verb: She doesn’t read.

Practice check

Choose the best answer for each sentence. Try without looking back at the review.

1. A shop assistant asks if you need anything. You say: “Yes, I need ___ notebook, please.”

2. Your brother’s school bus arrives at 8 o’clock. Send a message: “He ___ to school at 8 o’clock.”

3. You are choosing a drink for Mia at a café. Say: “She ___ coffee in the evening, so tea is better.”

4. You are making Friday plans. Ask about your friend’s teammates: “___ they play football on Fridays?”

5. Your classmates are comparing morning routines. Say: “We ___ eat breakfast before school.”

6. A neighbour asks when you study. Reply: “I do my homework ___ the evening.”

After the check

  • Review every answer you missed and say the corrected sentence aloud.
  • Write one positive sentence, one negative sentence, and one question about your usual day.
  • Make a short list of exam vocabulary to review before the next lesson.
Focused revision: articles, present simple, contractions, frequency adverbs, and time expressions.
